Three Chess House Restaurants venues close or relocate within days

Central's Shahrazad Lebanese Cuisine closes 31 July after seven years, Chutney Tandoor House shuts the same day to relocate, and Tung Chung's Maison du Mezze closed 26 July with plans to reopen elsewhere — all from the same operator group.

Market shiftSource: A Foodie World
Why it matters: A cluster of established Central and Tung Chung units are hitting the market at once — worth a quick scout of whether any fit a Flat Iron Burger or Steak King footprint.

Australian beef hit with 55% over-quota tariff into China

Beijing's Ministry of Commerce confirmed the additional levy after Australian shipments reached their annual import quota on 18 June, a move analysts say could cost Australian exporters up to US$1 billion a year.

Supply & costSource: Hong Kong Free Press
Why it matters: Even though Hong Kong sits outside the mainland quota, tightened mainland demand and exporter economics tend to ripple into regional pricing — worth stress-testing Flat Iron and Picanhas' beef COGS this quarter.

Delivery market narrows to a two-horse race between Keeta and Foodpanda

With Deliveroo gone from Hong Kong, Meituan-backed Keeta and Foodpanda are left to fight it out; Foodpanda reports 20%+ order growth while Keeta continues aggressive expansion, and analysts expect subsidy-driven price wars to ease as the field consolidates.

Delivery & platformsSource: SCMP
Why it matters: Fewer platforms competing usually means less promo pressure over time — worth reviewing Flat Iron Burger and Steak King online store commission and pricing terms while there's still leverage to negotiate.

HK dining spend up 14.2%, but the recovery is deeply split

Mastercard data show total dining spend rose 14.2% year on year in Q1; non-Chinese restaurants sit 7% above 2018 levels and fast food is up 11%, while traditional Chinese restaurants remain 24% below pre-pandemic revenue.

HK wine imports climb in value even as volumes soften

Wagyu demand keeps Asia-Pacific beef costs firm

Asia-Pacific holds roughly 49% of the global wagyu beef market and continues to drive growth, keeping premium beef and landed import costs elevated for restaurants across the region.
